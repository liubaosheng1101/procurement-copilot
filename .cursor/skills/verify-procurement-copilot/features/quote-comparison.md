# 报价比价

报价比价 turns mixed tax, freight, and payment terms into one plant-gate unit cost and an annual TCO, then ranks the offers. The built-in example ranks 深科电子（示例） first.

## Sub-features

- `quote-open` shows 多份报价横向比价 on first load and from the 报价比价 tab.
- `quote-blank-error` shows 计算未通过 when the blank offer rows are calculated.
- `quote-example` fills the three sample offers, the annual demand, and the baseline.
- `quote-rank` shows 深科电子（示例） as 综合最优 with unit cost, annual TCO, and the baseline saving.
- `quote-download` saves the result JSON from 下载结果 JSON.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Open the page. 报价比价 is the selected tab.
- Choose the 报价比价 tab again after visiting another calculator.

## Gotchas

- The first 开始计算 on a fresh form is expected to fail. The message `缺少必填字段: offers[0].supplier` is the proof of that path, not a harness bug.
- 载入示例 clears the previous result. The empty hint returns until 开始计算 is clicked again.
- The 资金成本率 input shows `6`, not `0.06`. Tax-rate inputs show `13` and `3`, not the fractions.
- Row ids are regenerated by 载入示例. Select suppliers with `input[data-path^="quote.r."][data-path$=".supplier"]` and `--nth`.
- Switching tabs drops the result. `browser goto` is the reset used here.
- KPI units sit inside the value with no space. Assert `10.83元`, not `10.83`.
- 复制结果 JSON is not part of this journey. Clipboard access is a poor proof. Use the download.
- Do not rank by the first row. 沪连精密（示例） is offer 1 and rank 2. 深科电子（示例） is rank 1 because annual demand is filled and TCO is the ranking metric.
